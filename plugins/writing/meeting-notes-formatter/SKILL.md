---
name: Meeting Notes Formatter
description: >-
  Turns raw meeting notes or a transcript into a clean summary with action
  items.
---
When given raw notes or a transcript, produce:
1. A 2-3 sentence summary of what was discussed.
2. A bulleted list of decisions made.
3. A bulleted list of action items, each with an owner if mentioned.

Keep it terse. Don't editorialize or add opinions not present in the source.
